About NexaFlow

A software company started from the business side of the desk.

Most software companies learn business from their customers. NexaFlow started the other way: years of running accounts and operations first, software second.

From the founder

For nearly seven years I have worked across accounts, operations, ERP systems, websites and apps. I have managed daily operations, closed the books at month end and run a business as a partner.

In manufacturing, in FMCG distribution and in service businesses, I kept seeing the same problems: stock that does not match, payments that come late, and reports that arrive after the decision is already made. Good people were losing hours every day to registers and Excel sheets.

I started NexaFlow to fix that. With today's AI tools, the daily work of a business can be automated at a price a normal Pakistani business can afford. That is what we build.
