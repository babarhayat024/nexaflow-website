FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does NexaFlow do?

NexaFlow sets up ERP software and accounting software in Pakistan, and builds custom business apps, mobile apps and websites. We work with shops, distributors, FMCG companies, manufacturers and service businesses.

I already use Odoo. Can you work with it?

Yes. We configure and improve an existing Odoo setup. If your Odoo plan allows outside connections, we can also connect a mobile app to it for your sales team.

I have no software at all. Can you still help?

Yes. We give you a complete system for sales, stock and accounts, with a mobile app and a web screen. You do not need any other software first.

Does it work on mobile and on a Windows computer?

Yes. The same system opens on an Android phone, in a web browser and as an app on a Windows computer.

Can you build a custom app for my business in Pakistan?

Yes. We build custom mobile, web and Windows apps around how your business works, such as order booking, field sales, delivery tracking, shop billing, stock and recovery. We first understand your process, then build only what you need.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of your business and what you need. You get the full price in writing, in rupees, before any work begins.
