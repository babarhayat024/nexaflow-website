FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does NexaFlow do?

NexaFlow builds manufacturing and ERP software for Pakistani factories, sets up accounting and POS software, and builds custom business apps, mobile apps and websites.

I already use other software. Can you work with it?

Yes. If your software allows outside connections through an API key, our apps work with it, for example for order booking or production entries. Your data stays in the system your office already uses.

I have no software at all. Can you still help?

Yes. We give you a complete system for sales, stock and accounts, with a mobile app and a web screen. You do not need any other software first.

Does it work on mobile and on a Windows computer?

Yes. The same system opens on an Android phone, in a web browser and as an app on a Windows computer.

Can you build a custom app for my business in Pakistan?

Yes. We build custom mobile, web and Windows apps around how your business works, such as order booking, field sales, delivery tracking, shop billing, stock and recovery. We first understand your process, then build only what you need.

Do you provide POS and invoicing software for shops?

Yes. We set up POS and invoicing software for shops in Pakistan: barcode billing, stock, credit customers and daily cash closing, on mobile, web or Windows. Our guide explains what a shop should look for.

What is the difference between ERP and accounting software?

Accounting software records your money: invoices, payments, ledgers and reports. An ERP runs the whole business, including sales, purchase, stock and production, and makes the accounting entries from that work. Read ERP vs accounting software.

How long does the setup take?

A small business with sales, stock and invoicing can usually go live in a few weeks. Factories and distributors take longer. Our guide on ERP implementation in Pakistan explains the time and the cost.

Do I need a licence for every worker?

No. Owners, managers and the office get full accounts. Foremen, store keepers, order bookers and delivery staff get simple accounts for their own work. See manufacturing software.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of your business and what you need. You get the full price in writing, in rupees, before any work begins.
