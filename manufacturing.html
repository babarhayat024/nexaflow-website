What we hear in every factory

  • Production is written in registers and typed into Excel at night, if at all.
  • Raw material leaves the store without a record against any order.
  • Wastage is found at month end, when it is too late to ask why.
  • Nobody is sure what one piece really costs.
  • Piece-rate wages are counted by hand every week.
  • Stock in cartons and loose pieces never matches the books.
  • Software licences are too expensive to give every supervisor a login, so the floor stays on paper.

Most software was made for factories in other countries, where every user pays a full licence and every process is the same. Pakistani factories work differently, so we design around how they actually run.

What it covers

One system from the store to the dispatch gate.

We set up the parts your factory needs, with your real products, recipes and cartons. Start small and add more as you grow.

Recipes and bills of materials

What goes into each product, in pieces, kilograms or metres, with the expected wastage.

Production orders

What to make, on which line, by when. Foremen see their own orders on a phone or a floor screen.

Raw material issue and return

Every issue from the store is recorded against a production order, so material cannot disappear between store and floor.

Wastage and scrap

Recorded on the day it happens, with a reason, so you see the real wastage before the month ends.

Piece-rate and daily wages

Pieces made by each worker or team turn into wages without counting by hand.

Job work for brands

Goods made for other companies, with their material and packing kept apart from your own stock.

Finished goods in cartons

Stock kept in pieces, dozens and cartons of different sizes, the way you actually pack and sell.

Quality checks

Checks at receiving, on the line and before dispatch, with results kept against the batch.

Gate passes

Material and goods going in and out of the gate are recorded and matched to orders.

Cost per piece

Material, wastage, labour and overheads added up, so you know what each product really costs.

Stock across stores

Raw material store, floor, finished goods and godowns, each with its own stock.

Reports on your phone

Today's production, wastage, stock and pending orders, for the owner wherever he is.

Questions factory owners ask

Do I need to buy a licence for every worker?

No. Owners, managers and the office get full accounts. Foremen, store keepers, gate staff and supervisors get simple accounts that show only their own work, so you do not pay a full licence for each person on the floor.

Can it handle job work for other brands?

Yes. Many Pakistani factories make goods for other companies, sometimes with the customer's own material or packing. We keep that material, production and delivery separate from your own stock, so both sides can be checked.

Is it only for big factories?

No. A small unit with one production line can start with recipes, production entries, stock and wastage, and add wages, quality checks and costing later.

We already use accounting or ERP software. Can it work with it?

Yes, if your software allows outside connections through an API key. Production and stock can be sent to the system your accounts team already uses.
