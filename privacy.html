Privacy policy

How we use the details you send us.

This page explains, in plain English, what NexaFlow collects through this website and what we do with it. Last updated: 15 November 2026.

Who we are

NexaFlow is a business software company in Pakistan, started by Babar Hayat. You can reach us at info@nexaflow.pk.

What we collect

We collect only what you type into our contact form: your name, your email address, your phone or WhatsApp number (optional) and your message. If you email us directly, we keep that email.

Why we collect it

We use your details only to read your message and reply to you. We do not sell your details. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

Who else handles your details

To run this website we use a few services, and they handle some data for us:

  • Web3Forms carries your contact form message to our email inbox.
  • Zoho Mail is where our email inbox is kept.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like any web host, it may record technical details of a visit, such as an IP address.
  • Google Fonts supplies the text style used on our pages, so your browser contacts Google to load it.
  • Google Analytics gives us anonymous visit statistics (see Cookies and statistics below).

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We do not share your details with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Cookies and statistics

This website does not set its own cookies. We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Google Analytics may set cookies in your browser and collects anonymous usage information such as pages viewed, approximate location and device type. We do not use it to identify you personally. You can block these cookies in your browser settings, or use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

How long we keep your details

We keep your message for as long as it is needed to answer you and to deal with any work that follows. After that, we delete it when we no longer need it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Write to info@nexaflow.pk and we will reply.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle your details, we will update this page and the date at the top.
