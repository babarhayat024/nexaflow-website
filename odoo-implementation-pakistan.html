Odoo implementation in Pakistan: how long it takes and what it costs

Before you start an Odoo project, know what the money is spent on and where the time goes. This guide explains both, for businesses in Pakistan.

By Babar Hayat, founder of NexaFlow · Updated 23 November 2026

Short answer

A small business that needs sales, stock and invoicing can usually go live on Odoo in a few weeks. A distributor with several warehouses and order bookers usually takes one to three months. A manufacturer with production and costing often needs three to six months. The cost has several parts: the Odoo subscription, the setup work, moving your data, training and support. A good implementer gives you the full price in writing, in rupees, before starting.

What Odoo is

Odoo is an ERP made of apps: Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Accounting, Manufacturing, Employees, Payroll and many more. You install only the apps you need. Odoo comes in two forms: Community, which is free and you host yourself, and the paid version, which Odoo hosts for you on Odoo Online or which you host yourself.

The parts of the cost

  • Odoo subscription. Odoo has a free plan for one app with unlimited users, a Standard plan with all apps, and a Custom plan. The paid plans are charged per user per month, and the price differs by country. Check the current price for Pakistan on odoo.com/pricing.
  • Which plan. Studio, more than one company in the same database and the external API (needed to connect a mobile app or another system) come with the Custom plan. Plan this early, because it changes the monthly cost.
  • Setup work. Configuring products, units and cartons, price lists, warehouses, taxes, the chart of accounts, approvals and print layouts.
  • Moving your data. Products, customers, suppliers, opening stock and opening balances. Clean data makes this fast; messy Excel sheets make it slow.
  • Training. Each person learns their own screens: order booker, warehouse, accountant, owner.
  • Custom work. New reports, special print layouts or new features. This is where costs grow, so keep it for what you really need.
  • Support after go-live. The first month always brings questions. Agree in advance who answers them.

How the time is spent

StageWhat happens
1. UnderstandWalk through how an order, a purchase and a payment move in your business today.
2. PlanA written plan: which apps, which settings, what data moves, who is trained, and the price.
3. ConfigureSet up the apps with your real products, units, price lists, warehouses and taxes.
4. Move dataImport products, customers, suppliers, opening stock and opening balances.
5. TestRun real orders from start to finish: order, delivery, invoice, payment, report.
6. Train and go liveTrain each team on its own screens, then switch on a chosen date, usually the first of a month.
7. SupportFix small problems in the first weeks and add what was left for later.

What makes an Odoo project slow and expensive

  • No one inside the business owns the project or has time to answer questions.
  • Product codes, units and customer names are different in every Excel sheet.
  • Trying to copy the old system exactly instead of using Odoo's standard way.
  • Asking for many custom reports before the basics are working.
  • Changing the plan halfway through.

How to keep the cost down

  • Start with Sales, Inventory and Invoicing or Accounting. Add Manufacturing or Payroll after the base is stable.
  • Use standard Odoo features first, then Odoo Studio, and custom code only as the last step.
  • Clean your product list and customer list before the project starts.
  • Choose one person in your business who decides and signs off each stage.

Things to set up for Pakistan

  • Rupee as the company currency, and your NTN and STRN on the company and invoices.
  • Sales tax and withholding tax rates as they apply to your business. Confirm these with your tax adviser before going live, because they change from time to time.
  • Units and cartons that match how you buy and sell, for example cartons of different sizes for different products.
  • Price lists for different customer groups, such as wholesale and retail.

Questions people ask

Can I use Odoo Community for free?

Yes, the Community version has no licence fee, but you pay for a server and for someone to install, update and back it up. Some apps and features are only in the paid version, so check that the apps you need are included.

Do I need an official Odoo partner?

No. You can work with any implementer. What matters is experience with businesses like yours, a written plan, clear pricing and support after go-live.

Can my sales team book orders on a mobile app that connects to Odoo?

Yes, if your Odoo plan allows outside connections through the external API. We build order booking apps that send orders into Odoo. See our services.

I already use Odoo but it does not work well. Can it be fixed?

Usually, yes. Most problems come from settings, product and unit setup or opening balances, not from Odoo itself. A review shows what to fix first.
